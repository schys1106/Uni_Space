How UniSpace Works

A simple four-step workflow from request to confirmation

01

Register & Login

Create your Class Representative account with your batch, semester and section details.

02

Find a Room

Filter by date, time slot, floor and room type, or tap a room directly on the Floor Map.

03

Submit Request

Provide the course name and teacher, then submit your booking request for review.

04

Get Approved

The Teacher / Admin panel approves or rejects your request instantly — no delays.

Interactive Floor Map Overview

Live status visualization across all CSE department floors — tap any green room to book it instantly
